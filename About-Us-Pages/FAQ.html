Frequently Asked Questions


Is the food served at McDonalds halal?

Answer: Yes, all McDonalds restaurants in Singapore are halal-certified by the Islamic Religious Council of Singapore.

How do I apply for a job at McDonalds?

Answer: View the 'Join Us' Page on the McDonalds Website to view the list of available positions and submit an online application to McDonalds.

How much are the Service Crew paid?

Answer: McDonald's Service Crew is paid on an hourly rate. The hourly rate varies with each restaurant.

What are the minimum qualifications I need to become a Restaurant Management Trainee?

Answer: You need a national ITE certificate in any discipline to apply for the position.

What are the working hours?

Answer: McDonalds offers flexible hours for their Service Crew depending on the needs of the restaurant and the availability of their Service Crew.

How do I get my money back if I was double-charged?

Answer: Check with the restaraunt to see if they are responsible. You can find the restaraunt contact information using the McDonalds Restaraunt Locator Page on their website. If not, check with your bank to rectify.

Where do I find the nutrition information for the McDonalds menu items?

Answer: Check the Nutrition Page on the McDonalds website.

Do you have vegetarian options?

Answer: No, McDonalds currently does not have vegetarian burgers or breakfast McMuffins.

Where does McDonalds get their beef from?

Answer: McDonalds has their beef sourced from Australia and New Zealand.

Does McDonalds have sugar-free syrup?

Answer: No, McDonalds does not have sugar-free syrup.

Where do I go to give feedback?

Answer: Click the 'Forms' button in the Home Page. When submitting the form, choose 'feedback' for the type of form you want to submit.

How do I find McDonalds' address?

Answer: You can go to our 'Contact Us' page to find McDonalds address.

How do I find out more about McDonalds?

Answer: You can find out more by clicking the links below the 'About Us' category on our Home Page.

Why did we decide to promote McDonalds?

Answer: We chose to promote McDonalds because of their great food and dedication to great customer service.
